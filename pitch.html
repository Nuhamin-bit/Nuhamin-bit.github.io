The problem I solve

AI has become both a shield and a target

A shield

Organizations use AI to work faster and defend themselves, from drafting to detecting fraud.

A target

Attackers misuse AI, and AI tools can leak data, make errors, or treat people unfairly.

The gap

Most organizations adopt AI faster than they can govern it or train people to use it well.

AI is only as safe as the people who build it and use it.

My research

Two questions: is AI governed, and are people ready?

AI governance

A framework scoring six standards

Five layers comparing NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, NIST CSF 2.0 Cyber AI Profile, OWASP and MITRE ATLAS, focused on AI agents.

AI fluency

The Cybersecurity & AI Capital Model

Measures Description, Delegation, Discernment and Diligence, plus cybersecurity literacy and workforce readiness. Published, SEINFORMS 2026.

Why I care about AI ethics

“At the Embassy of France, I managed programs on cyber-facilitated gender-based violence. I saw how technology can harm vulnerable people.”

That is why I bring a people-first lens to AI: checking for bias, protecting privacy, keeping humans in control of important decisions, and being transparent with the people AI affects.
